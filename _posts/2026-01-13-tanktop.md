Bello!
Here is the first piece of my final project!
It is titled: Light up top. The motivation behind this piece was to recreate my favorite
tank top from when I was a child that was full of butterflies and flowers and a velcro
caterpillar that you could move around the top.
Here is the revised paper prototype for this project-
Here is the alligator clips prototype-
and here are two images of my piece working like it is supposed to-
Two tips I would give my past self related to this project are:
1. To use more conductive thread than I think I need because my top is stretchy so
it'll be helpful in keeping it from breaking the circuit
2. not use more than 4 LEDs!

List of materials used:
1. Babydoll top
2. LEDs
3. conductive thread
4. embroidery floss
5. needle
6. battery holder
7. coin battery
8. felt
9. conductive fabric
