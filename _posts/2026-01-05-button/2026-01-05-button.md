Bello again,
For this assignment I had to write a code where a different light would turn on each time
I would press a button after previously having it unpressed. We also used the while function
to ensure that the same light does not turn on immediately after being just turned on even though
the selection of lights is randomized.
3 tips I would give my past self-
1. make pressed and unPressed state variables instead of going with HIGH and LOW because that gets
reallyyyyyyy confusing
2. figure out how to actually use the alligator clips to connect my button to the arduino again-- I had a little problem with this one.
3. AND add tons of comments because it helps you organize your code and figure out what you did where without needed to decipher complex code.
