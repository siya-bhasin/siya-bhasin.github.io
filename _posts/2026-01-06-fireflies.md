Bello!
For this project, we mimicked 3 different firefly blinking patterns on pins on our Arduino by writing a code for it
in Arduino IDE.
The 3 firefly blinking patterns we recreated were-
1. Big Dipper: brightness increases by 30 every 0.1 seconds till max brightness every 3 seconds
2. Flash Bulb: max brightness for 0.5 seconds every 4 seconds
3. Blue Ghost: brightness randomly goes up or down by 50

One tip I would give to someone attempting this project for the first time would be to
think about how they can use operators to minimize the writings in their code because it helps 
make things less complicated and easier to go through when debugging!
