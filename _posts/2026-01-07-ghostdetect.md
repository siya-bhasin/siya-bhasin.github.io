Bello!
For this assignment, we worked with light and temperature sensors to turn on all the red LEDs in our arduino whenever 
the sensors sensed the bottom 25% threshold of temperature and light values (i.e. when it was dark and cold) to detect
that a ghost was in the area. I don't believe in this because I tried my entire childhood to befriends ghosts and 
it never worked so I don't think they exist but ig a girl can dream.
Tip I would give my past self is to ALWAYS DOUBLE CHECK that I have connected the alligator clips to the right place
because I short circuited my temperature sensor :(
