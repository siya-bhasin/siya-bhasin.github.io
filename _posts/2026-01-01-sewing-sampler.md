A **tip** past me would highly benefit from would be tracing my sewing path as accurately as possible with a pencil before going over it with needle and thread. None of the YouTube tutorials I watched had drawn over their paths and so I assumed that was the normal thing to do-- not realizing they were professionals at this-- which led to a few spacing inconsistencies. However, once I started tracing my intended path with a pencil, things started looking better for my sampler. 
A **trick** I would suggest is to tie the end of my blanket stitch to one of the loops on the running stitch around my sewing because that was less complicated than figuring out how to tie it to one of the loops on my blanket stitch.
